Make the website feel bespoke.

A visual reset plan to replace the thesis-like homepage with a concise, proof-led studio story supported by a small set of intentional ImageGen assets.

The decision

Reduce the homepage to one promise, three proof signals, three selected projects, one four-step process, and one direct invitation to start a conversation.

New homepage journey

01 Hero

One clear promise, one CTA, one editorial visual.

02 Proof

Custom platforms, mobile-first delivery, clear ownership.

03 Work

Three case studies with status and evidence.

04 Offer

Discover the system, build the product, support handover.

05 Process

Four steps with tangible outputs, not abstractions.

06 Close

“Bring us the workflow that keeps breaking.”

What moves off the homepage

The long manifesto, compiler demo, comparison matrix, repeated philosophy sections, and secondary capability explanations move to About or Process. They remain available without forcing every visitor to read them.

ImageGen asset set

Use the built-in ImageGen tool one asset at a time. Keep interfaces, icons, logos, and factual diagrams code-native.

Case-study variants
Generate a consistent visual family for education, operations, and commerce. Keep faces secondary; make the workflow and product context the subject. Do not invent client logos, testimonials, metrics, or private records.

Copy limits

  • Hero headline: 12 words maximum.
  • Supporting copy: 24 words maximum.
  • Section introductions: 35 words maximum.
  • Cards: one sentence plus a status label.
  • Replace guarantees with observable evidence.

Implementation sequence

  1. Rewrite structure and copy.
  2. Remove or relocate the terminal and repeated manifesto.
  3. Generate and test the hero visual on mobile.
  4. Rebuild case-study cards around existing product evidence.
  5. Align Services, Work, Process, and Contact.
  6. Check 375px, 768px, and 1440px widths.

Three-pass acceptance test

Comprehension: Can a visitor explain what Sankofa builds after the first screen?

Credibility: Does every visual reinforce a real capability or published project boundary?

Conversion: Can a mobile visitor reach a case study or start an enquiry in under one minute?
